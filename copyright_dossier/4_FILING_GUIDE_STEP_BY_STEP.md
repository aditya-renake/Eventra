3. Post via Speed Post to:

### ⏳ Step 6: Tracking & Grant of Registration
1. **Mandatory 30-Day Waiting Period:** Under Rule 70(9), a mandatory 30-day waiting period begins for any third-party objections.
2. **Examination:** The copyright examiner reviews your source code and Statement of Operation.
3. **ROC Certificate Issuance:** Once cleared, an official **ROC (Register of Copyrights) Certificate** with the Government of India seal will be issued digitally and downloadable from your portal!
